Is this a subscription?

No — one-time payment, lifetime license for a single Snowflake account. No renewals, no recurring charges.

What if my download doesn't start, or I closed the page too soon?

Email vizcanvas@gmail.com with your Razorpay payment ID (shown on the confirmation page) and we'll get your download to you directly.

Do I need to uninstall the trial first?

No — the Full Edition installs as its own application, separate from the trial. You can run both, or remove the trial anytime with its uninstall script.

Does my data ever leave my Snowflake account?

Never. Full Edition runs on the same privacy model as the trial — reads only SNOWFLAKE.ACCOUNT_USAGE, no outbound network calls.
