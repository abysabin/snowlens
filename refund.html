Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains refunds and cancellations for purchases of the SnowLens Full Edition from VizCanvaz. SnowLens is a digital software product delivered by download, so please read the following carefully before purchasing.

1. Nature of the product

The SnowLens Full Edition is a one-time digital software license, delivered electronically (by download link and/or email) immediately after successful payment. There is no physical product and no shipment.

2. Cancellations

Because access is delivered instantly after payment, orders cannot be cancelled once the download has been delivered. If you have not yet received or accessed your download, you may request a cancellation by contacting us as soon as possible at the email below, and we will cancel and refund the order where the software has not yet been accessed.

3. Refunds

As a one-time digital license delivered immediately, purchases are generally non-refundable once access has been delivered.

However, we want you to be satisfied. If you experience a genuine technical issue that prevents the Software from working as described, and we are unable to resolve it for you, contact us within 7 days of purchase at vizcanvas@gmail.com with your Razorpay payment reference and a description of the issue. We will review every such request in good faith on a case-by-case basis, and where appropriate we will issue a full or partial refund.

4. How approved refunds are processed

Where a refund is approved, it will be issued to the original payment method through Razorpay. Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days of approval, though the exact time for the amount to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

5. What is not eligible for a refund

  • Change of mind after the software has been downloaded and accessed.
  • Snowflake compute costs incurred while running the Software (these are billed by Snowflake, not by VizCanvaz, and are outside our control).
  • Issues caused by modifying the software in violation of the license terms.

6. How to request a refund or cancellation

Email vizcanvas@gmail.com with your Razorpay payment reference (shown on your payment confirmation) and a short description of the reason. We aim to respond to all refund and cancellation requests within 2 business days.

7. Contact

For any billing questions, see our About & Contact page or email vizcanvas@gmail.com.
