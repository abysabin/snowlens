Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VizCanvaz ("VizCanvaz", "we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with SnowLens (the "Software") and the website snowlens.vizcanvaz.com. We have designed SnowLens so that your Snowflake data never reaches us in the first place.

1. The most important point first

SnowLens runs entirely inside your own Snowflake account. It reads only Snowflake's own metadata views (QUERY_HISTORY, WAREHOUSE_METERING_HISTORY, QUERY_ATTRIBUTION_HISTORY) and makes no outbound network calls. Your query text, query results, table data, and business data never leave your Snowflake account and are never transmitted to, received by, or visible to VizCanvaz.

2. Information we do collect

We only collect the limited information needed to sell and support the Software:

  • Billing information — when you purchase the Full Edition, our payment processor (Razorpay) shares with us the details needed to fulfil your order, typically your name, email address, and payment reference/ID. We do not receive or store your full card number or bank details — those are handled entirely by Razorpay.
  • Communications — if you email us for support, we retain that correspondence and your email address in order to respond and provide support.
  • Basic website analytics — our website is a static site. If we use any privacy-respecting analytics or our host records standard server logs (e.g. IP address, browser type), these are used only to understand aggregate traffic and keep the site secure.

3. How we use this information

  • To process your payment and deliver your Full Edition license and download.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • To send you essential transactional messages about your purchase (not marketing, unless you opt in).
  • To comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

4. Payment processing

Payments are processed by Razorpay, a third-party payment provider. When you make a payment, your card and banking details are collected and processed directly by Razorpay under their own privacy policy and security standards (PCI-DSS). We never see or store your full payment credentials. Please review Razorpay's privacy policy for details on how they handle your payment data.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary:

  • With our payment processor (Razorpay) to complete transactions.
  • With service providers that help us operate (e.g. email delivery), bound to protect your data.
  • Where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

6. Data retention

We retain billing and correspondence records for as long as needed to provide support, honour your license, and meet legal and tax obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, subject to our legal record-keeping obligations. To make a request, email us at the address below.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we hold. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at vizcanvas@gmail.com. Full business contact details are on our About & Contact page.
