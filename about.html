About SnowLens

SnowLens is a free Snowflake observability app built by Abin Alex under the VizCanvaz brand. It installs directly into your own Snowflake account, surfaces query-performance and cost anomalies using 9 detection rules, and never sends any data outside your environment.

SnowLens runs on Streamlit-in-Snowflake, requires no external infrastructure, and makes no outbound network calls. It is completely free — no payment, no signup, no expiry.
