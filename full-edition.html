FAQ

Before you buy

Is this a subscription?

No — one-time payment, lifetime license for a single Snowflake account. No renewals, no recurring charges.

What if my download email doesn't arrive?

Email vizcanvas@gmail.com with your Razorpay payment ID (shown on the confirmation page) and we'll send it directly.

Do I need to uninstall the trial first?

No — the Full Edition installs into its own database and role, separate from the trial. You can run both, or uninstall the trial with sql/99_uninstall.sql whenever you like.

Does my data ever leave my Snowflake account?

Never. Full Edition runs on the same privacy model as the trial — reads only SNOWFLAKE.ACCOUNT_USAGE, no outbound network calls.
