Delivery Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SnowLens is a digital software product. There is no physical shipment. Everything is delivered electronically over the internet — there are no couriers, no shipping charges, and no physical delivery timelines.

1. How the product is delivered

The SnowLens Full Edition is delivered as a downloadable software package. Immediately after a successful payment, you are taken to a confirmation page with your download, and/or a download link and setup instructions are sent to the email address you provided at checkout.

2. Delivery timeline

Delivery is instant to a few minutes in the vast majority of cases. If your download or email has not arrived within 24 hours of a successful payment, please contact us and we will deliver it to you directly.

3. Delivery charges

There are no delivery or shipping charges. The price you pay at checkout is the complete price for the license; delivery is electronic and free.

4. International delivery

Because delivery is entirely digital, the product is available worldwide with no difference in delivery method or time based on your location. An internet connection is all that is required to receive and install it.

5. Didn't receive your download?

First, check your email spam/junk folder. If you still cannot find it, email vizcanvas@gmail.com with your Razorpay payment reference (shown on your payment confirmation) and we will re-send your download link promptly.

6. Contact

For any delivery questions, see our About & Contact page or email vizcanvas@gmail.com.
