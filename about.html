About VizCanvaz

VizCanvaz builds developer tools for the Snowflake Data Cloud. Our product, SnowLens, is a Snowflake observability app that installs directly into a customer's own Snowflake account and surfaces query-performance and cost anomalies — analysing only Snowflake's own usage metadata, so no customer data ever leaves the account.

SnowLens is offered as a free Trial Edition and a paid Full Edition. It runs on Streamlit-in-Snowflake, requires no external infrastructure, and makes no outbound network calls. We are an India-based software business serving customers in India and internationally.
